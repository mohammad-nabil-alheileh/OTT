# Pre-, Mid-, and Post-Roll Ads

These are the **three positions** where an **ad break** can appear relative to the **content** (the movie or episode).

```
[Pre-roll] ── content ── [Mid-roll] ── content ── [Mid-roll] ── content ── [Post-roll]
```

| Position      | When it plays                                          | Why it matters                                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pre-roll**  | **Before** the content starts                          | Makes **"stream minute 5 is not content minute 5"** true                      |
| **Mid-roll**  | **In the middle**, at specific points (**cue points**) | What you **block seeking through**                                             |
| **Post-roll** | **After** the content ends                             |                                                                                |

## Why It Matters

**Each position behaves differently in the player.**

> **Resume and "Continue Watching" bugs** usually come from a **mid-roll** sitting between the saved position and the start.
