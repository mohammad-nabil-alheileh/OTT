# OTT vs Broadcast

## Broadcast

**Broadcast** is the traditional way of delivering TV. A station sends **one signal** over the air, cable, or satellite, and every TV tuned in receives the **same stream at the same moment**.

> The station doesn't know who is watching.

## OTT (Over-The-Top)

**OTT** means video delivered **over the internet**. Each viewer's device **requests** the video from a server, so every viewer gets their own stream.

## Comparison

| Aspect            | Broadcast                         | OTT                                            |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Delivery**      | One signal to everyone            | Separate stream per viewer, over the internet  |
| **Viewer data**   | Almost none                       | Detailed (what, when, how long)                |
| **Interactivity** | Very limited                      | Full: search, resume, profiles                 |
| **Device**        | TV with a tuner or set-top box    | Any internet device, including a TV app        |

## Key Takeaway

- **Broadcast** = *push*: one signal, everyone watches the same thing at the same time.
- **OTT** = *pull*: each device asks for its own stream, whenever the viewer wants it.
